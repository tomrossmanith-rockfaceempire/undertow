    How to dive

    1. Read the prompt. You get 3 seconds to think before the answer box opens, then 20 seconds to type.
    2. Type any correct answer. Wrong guesses cost nothing, so keep trying until the timer runs out. Your first accepted answer ends the round.
    3. Rarer answers sink you deeper. Each point is 10 meters. A Shallows answer scores 10, a Leviathan scores 100. Seven Leviathans reach the trench floor at 7,000 m.
    4. Share your dive. Everyone gets the same 7 prompts each day. Your result shows colors, not answers, so nothing gets spoiled.
    5. Or dive together. Play with friends gives the host a room code. Everyone joins on their own device, answers the same prompts at the same time, and sees the standings after each one.

    A new dive opens at midnight Eastern Time. The timer keeps running if you switch tabs, so looking things up won't help.
